# Poeta

**The AI poetry studio for the moments that matter most. Real poetry, written with you, not for you.**

[poetaai.com](https://poetaai.com)

---

## What it is

Poeta is co-creation, not generation. You bring the moment (a wedding toast, a 2 AM eulogy, a private vow, a letter you've been afraid to write), and Poeta scaffolds the structure, suggests phrasing, and iterates with you until the poem sounds like you wrote it. Because you did.

Not a "type a prompt, get a poem" generator. Not a Hallmark card. Not the AI cliche slop that floods the internet. A studio that respects that some words have to be earned.

## Stack

Built with TypeScript, Node, local MLX inference. Deep memory of your previous pieces so subsequent work can reference your voice. Founder licenses available at [poetaai.com](https://poetaai.com).
